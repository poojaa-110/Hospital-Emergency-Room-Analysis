# 🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Analysis

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes 9,216 patient visits from **April 2023 to October 2024** using **SQL and Power BI**.

The goal is to Emergency departments need to balance unpredictable patient demand with limited staff, treatment capacity, and specialist resources. A high patient flow can create longer waits, delays in care, pressure on referral departments, and weaker patient experience.


## 📊 Key Analysis

The analysis answers questions such as:

- How many patients visit the emergency room?
- What percentage of patients are admitted?
- When is patient demand highest?
- Which hours and days experience higher patient volume?
- What is the average patient waiting time?
- What percentage of patients are seen within 30 minutes?
- Which departments receive the highest number of referrals?
- What are the major patient demographic patterns?
- What does the patient satisfaction data indicate?
- Which operational areas require further attention?

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **SQL** – Data analysis and KPI calculations
- **Power BI** – Dashboard and visualization
- **CSV** – Source dataset

## 🔍 Key Findings

- **9,216** patient visits were analyzed.
- Around **50%** of patients were admitted.
- Average waiting time was approximately **35 minutes**.
- Around **41%** of patients were seen within 30 minutes.
- General Practice had the highest recorded referral volume.
- Patient demand varied by month, weekday, and hour.
- Satisfaction data contains many zero values and requires careful interpretation.
